# L&H Poultry - process notes

Half-written doc someone started in Google Docs a while back and never
finished. Pasting what's here.

## Processing floor
No written SOP. New hires shadow Marcus for "a few days" until he says
they're ready. Marcus has been here 11 years and is the only one who knows
the full USDA compliance checklist by heart - it's not written down anywhere
that we could find.

## Ordering feed
Whoever notices we're running low calls Tri-County and orders "the usual."
No standing order, no reorder point, no written spec of quantities. This is
why we had a scramble in October when both the usual orderer (Dave) and the
backup (Marcus) were out the same week - almost ran out.

## Delivery routing
Driver picks the route each morning based on "however feels right." No
route optimization, no software, just experience. Works ok with only 4
vehicles but nobody could step in and do it cold.

## Invoicing customers
Linda does this manually in QuickBooks after checking the delivery log
against a paper clipboard. Sometimes the clipboard gets misplaced for a
day or two and invoicing slips. No formal invoice review or approval step
before it goes out (or before we chase someone for payment).

## Payroll / scheduling
Scheduling is a shared Google Sheet that the shift leads edit directly -
no version history reviewed, occasional double-bookings. Payroll itself
goes through ADP but hours are hand-keyed from time cards, not clocked
digitally.

## Onboarding new employees
No written checklist. HR paperwork, safety training, and system access
(QuickBooks, Slack, etc.) all happen "whenever someone gets around to it."
We've had at least one new hire on the floor for two weeks before getting
added to payroll properly (caught it, but could have been bad).

## Equipment maintenance
Reactive only - fix it when it breaks. No preventive maintenance schedule
for the delivery vans or processing equipment. (See the van #2 transmission
and AC repairs in Jan/March - both vehicles are getting up there in miles.)

## What IS documented
- USDA facility inspection prep checklist (printed, taped to the wall in
  the break room, not digitized)
- Basic cash handling procedure for the small retail counter
